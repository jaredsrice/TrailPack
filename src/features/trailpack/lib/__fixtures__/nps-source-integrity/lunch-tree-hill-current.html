Lunch Tree Hill

Grand Teton National Park

This short, interpretive trail takes hikers to the top of a hill with views of Willow Flats and the Teton Range. From Jackson Lake Lodge, follow the signs for Lunch Tree Hill. This easy hike gives visitors some of the more spectacular views in the valley.

20-45 Minutes
Lunch Tree Hill is an easy, 0.5 mi (0.8 km) lollipop loop trail with 110 ft (30 m) of elevation gain.

Accessibility Information
Lunch Tree Hill is an easy, 0.5 mi (0.8 km) lollipop loop trail with 110 ft (30 m) of elevation gain and an average slope of 10%. The trail is paved but sits on a steep incline.
