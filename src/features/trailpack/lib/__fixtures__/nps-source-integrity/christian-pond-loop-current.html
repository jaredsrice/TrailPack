Christian Pond Loop

Grand Teton National Park

The Christian Pond Loop is a trail that takes hikers through a variety of habitats. Starting from Jackson Lake Lodge, the trail loops the pond and travels through marsh habitat. The trail then climbs a forested ridge and offers views of the Teton Range. When traveling through dense willows, be alert for wildlife.

Accessibility Information
The Christian Pond Loop is an easy, 3.5 mi (5.6 km) loop hike with 490 ft (150 m) of elevation gain and an average slope of 6%. The trail is initially steep leaving the trailhead before it becomes a gentle level path.
