Heron Pond - Swan Lake Loop Trail

1-3 Hours

The Heron Pond-Swan Lake Loop is an easy, 3.1 mi (5 km) loop hike with 320 ft (100 m) of elevation gain.

The Heron Pond-Swan Lake Loop is an easy, 3.1 mi (5 km) loop hike with 320 ft (100 m) of elevation gain and an average slope of 5%. While the trail is relatively level, trail obstacles, such as exposed roots and rocks, exist.
