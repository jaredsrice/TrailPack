Take an easy-moderate hike to Grand View Point for spectacular mountain and lake views. Leaving from the Jackson Lake Lodge, visitors will hike north around Christian Ponds. At the Emma Matilda split, follow signs for Grand View Point. Here, the trail begins to climb, topping out with panoramic views of the Teton Range and Jackson Hole. Follow the same trail back. To extend the hike, continue the loop around Christian Ponds.

Grand View Point from Jackson Lake Lodge 

Grand View Point is a moderate, 5.8 mi (9.3 km) RT out and back hike with 950 ft (290 m) of elevation gain.

Grand View Point from the Grand View Point Trailhead

Grand View Point is an easy, 2.6 mi (4.2 km) RT out and back hike with 740 ft (230 m) of elevation gain.

Accessibility Information

Grand View Point from Jackson Lake Lodge 

Grand View Point is a moderate, 5.8 mi (9.3 km) RT out and back hike with 950 ft (290 m) of elevation gain and an average slope of 13%.
 

Grand View Point from the Grand View Point Trailhead

Grand View Point is an easy, 2.6 mi (4.2 km) RT out and back hike with 740 ft (230 m) of elevation gain and an average slope of 9%.
 

The trail includes steep switchbacks, narrow sections passing through dense underbrush, and exposed rocks and roots.
