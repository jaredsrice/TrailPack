Hike up Signal Mountain for spectacular views of the Teton Range and Jackson Hole. Starting from the Signal Mountain Lodge, cross the Teton Park Road and follow the signs to the trailhead. From there, the trail winds upwards through the forest to the summit of Signal Mountain where hikers are met with panoramic views of the Teton Range and Jackson Hole.

Accessibility Information
The Signal Mountain Trail is a moderate, 6.7 mi (10.8 km) RT out and back hike with 980 ft (300 m) of elevation gain and an average slope of 8%. The trail is narrow and winding with exposed rocks and roots throughout.
