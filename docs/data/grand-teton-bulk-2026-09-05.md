The weather coordinates come from the [official NPS trailhead markers](https://www.nps.gov/grte/planyourvisit/upload/Grand-Teton-Trailhead-Map.json). The final column is their offset from reviewed route/access geometry, not GPS accuracy or a safety threshold. Alpine routes explicitly warn that weather at the lower access point does not describe the higher route.

No new DEM-derived gain is claimed. The downloaded trail layer provides 2D geometry and lengths; computed gain stays unknown. Official NPS gain is displayed unchanged. NPS page-level duration ranges can cover more than one approach and are not invented per-variant travel estimates. The combined Two Ocean/Emma page itself uses Strenuous in Duration and Moderately Strenuous in Accessibility; the profile retains its primary Duration wording and shows the official terrain paragraph without rewriting that discrepancy.

## Access and day-hike limits

[NPS construction information](https://www.nps.gov/grte/planyourvisit/road-construction.htm), reviewed September 5, reports the old Death Canyon road and trailhead closed to all traffic, including pedestrians. The new trailhead will change hike distances. Phelps Overlook, Phelps Lake from Death Canyon, Death Canyon junction, Static Peak Divide, and Open Canyon retain a visible dated restriction. Their published old-start metrics must not be used for an alternate or future relocated approach. Re-review before clearing these notes or changing the start.

Holly Lake, Paintbrush–Cascade, Lake Solitude, Hurricane Pass, Surprise–Amphitheater, Garnet Canyon, Static Peak Divide, Marion Lake, and Open Canyon retain alpine/day-hike limitations. Garnet includes NPS boulder-field scrambling, not summit climbing. Static Peak stops at the divide, not the summit; Open Canyon stops at Mount Hunt Divide, not an added ridge climb. No overnight or climbing equipment coverage is implied.
